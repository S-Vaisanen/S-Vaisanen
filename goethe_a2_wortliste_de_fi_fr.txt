GOETHE-ZERTIFIKAT A2 – WORTLISTE: DEUTSCH | SUOMI | FRANÇAIS
=============================================================
Source of the German vocabulary: Goethe-Institut, "Goethe-Zertifikat A2 – Wortliste" (2016)
https://www.goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/de/Goethe-Zertifikat_A2_Wortliste.pdf
Finnish and French translations added (A2-level, most common meaning as used in the list).

HOW TO READ
- Format of each line:  German | Finnish | French
- German nouns: article + noun, plural ending ("-" = no change, "¨" = umlaut, (Sg.) = singular only, (Pl.) = plural only).
- German verbs: infinitive, 3rd person singular, perfect.
- French nouns: le / la / un / une shows the gender. When the article contracts to l',
  the gender is given at the end: (masculine) / (feminine). The same is done for nouns
  that are normally used with "les".
- Finnish has no articles or grammatical gender.
- ";" separates different meanings, "/" separates alternatives.
